Opening Hours & Facility Rules

- Badminton Courts 1 & 2: open daily, 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM.
- Football Turf A: open daily, 6:00 AM - 11:00 PM.
- Tennis Court 1: open daily, 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM.
- Multipurpose Room: open daily, 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM (for events, workshops, indoor games).
- The facility is closed on major national holidays; check the notice board or app for
  holiday closures.
- Non-marking shoes are mandatory on all indoor and turf surfaces.
- Outside food is not allowed on the football turf or inside the multipurpose room.
- Each booking slot includes a 10-minute buffer after the hour for changeover; please
  vacate the court/turf promptly at the end of your slot.
