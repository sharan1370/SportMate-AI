Pricing & Payment

- Badminton courts: INR 300 per hour.
- Tennis court: INR 500 per hour.
- Football turf: INR 1200 per hour (minimum booking 60 minutes; most bookings are 90 minutes).
- Multipurpose room: INR 800 per hour (minimum booking 2 hours for events).
- Payment can be made online at the time of booking, or in cash at the facility
  reception before the session starts.
- Peak hours (6 PM - 9 PM on weekdays, and all day on weekends) carry a 10% surcharge
  on the base hourly rate.
- Members with an active membership get a flat 15% discount on all bookings, applied
  automatically at checkout.
