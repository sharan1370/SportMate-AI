Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

- Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 2 hours before the slot start time.
- Cancellations made within 2 hours of the slot start time are non-refundable.
- Rescheduling is allowed once per booking, subject to availability, and must also be
  done at least 2 hours before the original slot start time.
- No-shows (not cancelling and not showing up) are treated the same as a late cancellation
  and are non-refundable.
- Refunds for eligible cancellations are processed within 3-5 business days to the
  original payment method.
