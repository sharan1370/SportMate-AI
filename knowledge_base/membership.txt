Membership

- Monthly membership: INR 1500, includes 15% discount on all bookings and priority
  access to peak-hour slots (can book up to 7 days in advance vs. 3 days for non-members).
- Annual membership: INR 15000 (equivalent to 2 months free vs. monthly), same benefits
  as monthly membership.
- Membership can be purchased at the reception or through the booking app.
- Membership is non-transferable and tied to the registered phone number.
- Membership does not include equipment rental discounts.
