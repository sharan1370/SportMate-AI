Equipment Rental

- Badminton rackets: INR 50 per racket per session. Shuttlecocks: INR 20 each.
- Football: INR 100 per session (deposit of INR 500 required, refunded on return).
- Tennis rackets: INR 100 per racket per session. Tennis balls: INR 150 per can of 3.
- Equipment can be reserved in advance when booking a slot, or rented on arrival subject
  to availability.
- All rented equipment must be returned at the facility reception immediately after the
  session. Damaged equipment will be charged at replacement cost.
